Daniel E. Mosquera P. came to Canada at the age of nine, in 2004, and has lived in Edmonton for most of his life. He has been making photographs for more than ten years.

He holds a deep sense of reverence for the seasons. He likes trees because they show us temperance, patience and fortitude, the attributes it takes to live in this climate. His work follows how perspective changes with the seasons, and how familiar places can look different under different light.
