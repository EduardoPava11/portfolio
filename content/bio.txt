Daniel E. Mosquera P. came to Canada at the age of nine, in 2004, and has lived in Edmonton for most of his life. He is an engineer by training, with a bachelor's degree in materials engineering from the University of Alberta (2017), and has been making photographs for more than ten years.

His engineering gave him a fundamental understanding of what light is and how it behaves: reflection, refraction, the way a material answers the light that falls on it. Photography is how he expresses what he finds interesting in his environment, and light is the medium. Used deliberately, it pushes the boundaries of our limited vision.

He holds a deep sense of reverence for the seasons. He likes trees because they show us temperance, patience and fortitude, the attributes it takes to live in this climate. His work follows how perspective changes with the seasons, and how familiar places can look different under different light.
